MatchSmith

Mobile Roguelite Solo · Godot

Summary

A blacksmith roguelite where matching pieces raises the Power, Durability and Sharpness of a weapon. The point is not only to clear the board. It is to decide how far you can push an order before you run out of moves.

What I did

  • Design document, run structure and economy
  • Quality, scoring and upgrade systems
  • UI and UX across every screen
  • Characters, icons and visual direction

Highlights

Customers remember the quality you usually give them. If you keep making excellent weapons, that eventually becomes normal to them. I wanted doing good work to build a relationship, while also making that relationship harder to maintain.

Core mechanic

Each piece has a purpose

Three pieces raise one weapon stat each. Tongs raise all three, while flame helps whichever stat is currently falling behind.

Customers

Choose whose order to take

Each order shows the customer, weapon requirements, move limit and reward. Harder customers pay more, but failing them also affects the relationship.

Progression

Bring customers into the workshop

Better work raises your reputation and brings in more customers. Between runs you can review customers, weapons and permanent unlocks from the workshop.

An Evening with the Unhallowed

GMTK Game Jam 2022 · 48 hours Game Design · Character Art · UI

What the game is

A 2D platformer made for the GMTK 2022 theme, roll of the dice. The player is followed by a ghost knight whose shield can be placed in the air and used as a platform.

How it plays

  • Collect dice faces scattered through each level
  • Clear enemies and solve platforming to reach the ones you still need
  • Spend skill points to upgrade your attributes
  • A stage opens only once you hold the required faces

What I did

Credited on the jam page for game design, character art and animation, and UI and icons. A team of seven, and the whole game was designed, built and shipped inside 48 hours.

Companion

The knight is part of your movement

The ghost knight follows the player, but his main purpose is not combat. You use his shield to reach places you could not reach alone.

Mechanic

Turn the shield into a platform

You can ask the knight to place his shield in mid-air, then stand on it. The limited number of shields makes each placement matter.

Level

Rolling for the route

Dice faces sit where the platforming is hardest, so reaching one is the puzzle. Collected faces read across the HUD.

The knight and player depend on each other. The knight follows the player, while the player uses his shields to move through the level. Because there are only a limited number, the player needs to think about where each platform is placed.
Dice faces are collected throughout the level and are used to unlock stages. This tied the jam theme directly to exploration and progression instead of making the result depend only on luck.

UX analysis

Before changing an interface, I first map how the current one works. For this exercise I traced Hearthstone's screens and grouped them by their main purpose: spending, managing cards and playing the game.

Information architecture

Hearthstone UI diagram

Every screen in the game laid out as a connected flow, from main menu through collection, crafting, arena, solo adventures and the ranked reward chest. Grouping the menu into money, card and play branches is what makes the structure legible: the diagram shows the shape of the product, not just its screens.

UI redesign · Hearthstone as Starcraft

For this exercise I kept Hearthstone's layout and interactions, then rebuilt the visual style around Starcraft. This let me focus on art direction without changing the information hierarchy or the way the screen is used.
